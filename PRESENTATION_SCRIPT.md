# Presentation script — Solana AI Wallet

Narration for the 8-slide deck. **About 1 minute 50** at a normal pace — comfortably inside two minutes.

**Read only the quoted lines.** Everything else is direction.

Short sentences, plain words. Where a line looks almost too simple, that is deliberate: the
arguments are strong on their own, and decorating them is what makes a pitch sound rehearsed.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover · 0:00–0:11

> Crypto wallets make beginners learn their vocabulary before they can send money.
>
> This is Solana AI Wallet. Say what you want in plain English. The blockchain checks it. You sign it.

*Pause. Let the title sit.*

---

## Slide 2 — The problem · 0:11–0:29

> Sending crypto means getting four things right at once — address, amount, fee, network. Get one wrong, and the money is gone.
>
> So let an AI do it. That is the dangerous answer. A model that can move money is a model whose mistakes cost money.

*Slow down for the last sentence. The whole project rests on it.*

---

## Slide 3 — The solution · 0:29–0:44

> So we kept the plain English and took the power away.
>
> The model suggests an amount and an address. That is all. It cannot read the chain, sign, or approve itself.
>
> When it is wrong, you get a refusal. Not a wrong transaction.

---

## Slide 4 — How it works · 0:44–1:00

> You type it. The model structures it. Then the app takes over.
>
> It checks the address, the amount, and your real balance. None of those checks ask the AI.
>
> Then the network runs it unsigned, so you see what would really happen.

*Move across the five steps as you speak. The colour changes at step three — that is where a
suggestion becomes verified fact.*

---

## Slide 5 — Five layers · 1:00–1:18

> Five layers sit between the model and your money.
>
> A strict schema. Independent checks against the chain. A simulation before you sign. And your wallet, holding the only key.
>
> We measured it. Zero signature requests while the AI worked. Exactly one, when approve was pressed.

*The last line is evidence, not a boast. Say it flat.*

---

## Slide 6 — Proof · 1:18–1:31

> A real transaction, made by this app, finalised on Solana Devnet.
>
> Ten million lamports moved. Five thousand lamport fee. No error. The balance matched the screen exactly.
>
> Nothing here is simulated.

---

## Slide 7 — Honest scope · 1:31–1:43

> It sends SOL, by chat or by form, and explains any transaction.
>
> No tokens. No swaps. No price data — it refuses those instead of inventing an answer. Every limit is shown in the app.

---

## Slide 8 — Close · 1:43–1:52

> The AI proposes. The chain verifies. You approve.
>
> Most AI wallets show a model that can move money. This one cannot.
>
> Thank you.

*A real beat between each of the first three lines.*

## For whoever records this

**Pace.** About 155 words a minute. The instinct under pressure is to rush. Don't — the pauses are
part of the timing, and the script is short enough to afford them.

**Tone.** Explaining something you built to someone whose opinion you respect. Not selling.

**Slow down three times.** The last line of slide 2. The "zero… exactly one" on slide 5. And the
three lines of slide 8.

**Say numbers as words.** "Ten million lamports", not "10,000,000". "Forty-four character address".
Text-to-speech mangles digits.

**Stumbles are fine** and sound human. Keep going. If you re-record a section, leave three seconds
of silence before restarting, so the cut is easy to find.

**Before the real take**, record ten seconds and play it back. Quiet room, microphone a hand's
width away and slightly to one side so your breath misses it.
